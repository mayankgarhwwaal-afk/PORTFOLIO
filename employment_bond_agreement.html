1. Bond Period & Compensation
  1. This Bond is valid for a minimum period of twelve (12) months from the date of joining.
  2. Starting monthly gross salary: ₹ ______/- per month, payable on the last working day of each month in accordance with the Payment of Wages Act, 1936.
  3. Salary revision shall be conducted annually, subject to performance appraisal; increment is not guaranteed and remains at the sole discretion of Management.
  4. The Employee shall be on probation for the first three (3) months. During probation, either party may terminate with seven (7) days' written notice.
  5. Upon early exit within the bond period without valid cause, the Employee shall repay an amount equivalent to two (2) months' gross salary as bond penalty to the Management.
2. Leave & Attendance Policy
  1. The Employee shall be entitled to Two (2) Paid Leaves per calendar month, accruing only after completion of three (3) continuous months of service with a minimum attendance of 90% or above every month.
  2. Unapproved absence beyond two (2) consecutive days without prior written intimation shall be treated as abandonment of service and may lead to immediate termination and forfeiture of dues.
  3. Leave must be applied for and approved by the Department Head at least 48 hours in advance, except in genuine medical emergencies supported by a certificate.
3. Dress Code & Personal Conduct
  1. The Employee shall wear the prescribed uniform issued by the Management at all times during duty hours; uniform must be clean, pressed, and properly maintained.
  2. Personal hygiene, grooming, and presentable appearance are mandatory and shall be inspected at shift commencement.
  3. Mobile phone usage for personal purposes during active duty hours is strictly prohibited.
4. Duty Hours & Overtime
  1. Regular duty shift: 8 hours per day, 6 days a week, as rostered by the Management. Weekly off shall be rotational as per operational requirements.
  2. Given the nature of the hospitality industry, the Employee agrees to work overtime when required due to occupancy, events, or emergencies. Overtime shall be compensated as per the Madhya Pradesh Shops & Establishments Act.
  3. Shift timings may be changed by the Management with a minimum of 24 hours' prior notice.
5. Misconduct & Immediate Termination
  1. Reporting to duty under the influence of alcohol or any intoxicant shall result in immediate termination without notice or salary for that period.
  2. Verbal abuse, disrespect, inappropriate behaviour, or any personal remarks directed at a guest shall result in summary dismissal without any salary entitlement.
  3. Disclosure of confidential information pertaining to the hotel, its guests, operations, or other staff to any third party shall result in immediate termination and may invite legal action under the Indian Contract Act, 1872.
  4. Theft, fraud, misappropriation of hotel property or cash shall be treated as a cognizable offence and reported to the appropriate police authority.
  5. Repeated misconduct (three or more instances) of any nature, even if minor, shall constitute a ground for summary dismissal after due inquiry.
  6. Abandoning duty without intimation for three (3) or more days, even if subsequently apologised for, shall be treated as willful desertion and termination notice shall be issued.
6. Notice Period & Resignation
  1. After completion of probation, either party must give 30 days' written notice before resignation or termination (other than summary dismissal).
  2. Management reserves the right to relieve the Employee immediately upon payment of one month's salary in lieu of notice.
7. General Clauses
  1. This Agreement is governed by the laws of India, including the Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act, 1946, the Payment of Wages Act, 1936, and applicable MP State Labour laws.
  2. All disputes arising under this Agreement shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts of Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh.
  3. The Employee confirms having received a copy of the Hotel's Code of Conduct and agrees to abide by it.
  4. This document, once signed, constitutes a legally binding agreement between both parties.
